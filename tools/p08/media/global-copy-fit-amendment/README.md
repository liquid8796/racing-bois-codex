reaching or exceeding the fixed button border. `changes.json` records the exact
three authored replacements and six immutable PNG hashes:

- DEU: `MOTORRAD UNBESCHÄDIGT` → `UNBESCHÄDIGT`.

- FRA: `MOTO EN PARFAIT ÉTAT` → `MOTO INTACTE`.

The key, pristine condition, disabled behavior, VI source, ENU `BIKE UNDAMAGED`,
ITA text, layout and typography remain unchanged. The prior pristine overlay
and union stay at their original paths; `before/` preserves provider and tool
bytes consumed by this revision. This is an explicit later amendment, not a
reinterpretation of the earlier native proof.

394-key locale catalogs, and one generated Career module. Its installer guards

the three declared changes, retain the earlier ENU correction, and check source,
render-evidence and template failure cases. All effective characters remain in
the existing 248-codepoint owned-font corpus. This does not prove the new strings
fit until the actual rerender measurements and images are inspected.
