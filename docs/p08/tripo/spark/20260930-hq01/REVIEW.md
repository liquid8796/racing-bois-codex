# Spark HQ01 source review

The actual saved Tripo GLB was imported through the task-owned pinned Blender
MCP on9888, saved, cold-reloaded and compared with its owned baseline buffers.
Five actual neutral PBR views were rendered; root inspected quarter, side and
rear. The source remains unaccepted and outside production Assets.

The copper/cream tank, brown saddle, black roadster frame, twin rear suspension
and right-side stacked silencers retain the source identity. The headlamp face,
mirrors and instrument faces are nearly blank reflective surfaces; the seat
and much of the steel/chrome response are excessively glossy. Surface seams,
small mechanics and rear/opposite-side details still differ or need comparison
with the master. One fused mesh has no wheel/steering articulation, rig or LODs.

`spark-v1.png` remains the locked multi-view master, and the unchanged large
`spark-v1-side.png` is the generation input. Technical preservation, high texture
resolution and completed API generation do not accept concept fidelity. A
separate parts reconstruction is the next working candidate for component
correction; it cannot inherit exact UV/geometry identity from this independent
textured generation.

The neutral cameras are uncalibrated full-object diagnostics. Physical scale,
rider contacts, meaningful runtime28k/14k/5.6k aggregate triangle derivatives,
1K–2K maps, Unity rendering and target hardware performance remain open.
