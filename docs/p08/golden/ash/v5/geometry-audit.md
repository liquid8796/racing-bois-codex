# Ash V5 geometry priorities — before editing

Compared the actual V4 portrait-baked.png and side-baked.png with the locked
ash-v2.png board. The locked references remain unchanged. These are visual
findings and construction measurements, not a fabricated similarity score.

1. **Goggles dominate the mismatch.** V4 reads as thick rectangular welding
   goggles. The concept has taller, more rounded individual apertures, a finer
   metal lip, distinct dark backing and a shaped bridge. The old source recipe
   used87×52mm outlines,3.5mm-radius metal tubes and a4.2mm lens bulge. Those
   authored dimensions are measured implementation inputs, not concept-derived
   absolute dimensions. V5 should narrow/round the apertures, increase their
   relative height and replace the hose-like rim with a manufactured lip.
2. **The strap loses physical contact/continuity.** The V4 profile shows a
   tapering strap end disappearing into the shell. Its old ellipse radii were
   fixed independently of the revised shell. The candidate should fit the
   actual outer shell with consistent clearance and real side attachment
   hardware. Back-view band visibility is unclear/inconsistent on the board;
   do not claim the unseen/ambiguous continuation is100% resolved.
3. **Helmet opening and lower silhouette.** V4 has a broad smooth cap and a
   nearly straight rear lower rim. The concept has a more shaped brow/ear
   opening, layered dark gasket/padding and clearer rear/nape separation.
   Preserve head clearance; reconstruct these surfaces, not a painted rim.
4. **Face identity needs structural work.** The current cheeks/jaw transition,
   narrow nasal bridge/tip, eyelid aperture, lip/philtrum contour and brow shape
   remain generic. Correct those landmarks in matched frontal/profile views
   before adding more pores or changing skin color. Surface noise cannot fix
   the underlying feature relationships.
5. **Hair and fine construction remain secondary open work.** The concept's
   wavy volume is not reproduced by a sparse fringe. Helmet hardware, strap
   closure, lens appearance and worn materials also remain unaccepted.

Immediate isolated V5 scope: rework goggles and their fit/strap, then inspect
front/profile/quarter renders. Keep V4 source/maps/FBX immutable and preserve
the existing rig/gameplay clips. No new ImageGen, cloud or install service is
used. A successful geometric check will not mark fidelity accepted.
