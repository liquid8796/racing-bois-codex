# Ash V8 Jacket02 retained study

This checkpoint keeps the owned PreEdit copy, rejected glossy Jacket01,
rougher Jacket02 and its separately saved render state. The original unowned
V7R2 source and its backup are preserved and excluded from this publication.
The unchanged locked reference is `ArtSource/Concepts/P08/Golden/ash-v2.png`.

Actual body/front/profile renders show a better charcoal/amber palette, but the
garment remains smooth and inflated, the collar bulky, and the stripes and folds
different from the reference. Face, hair, gloves and boots still need their own
reference review. **Jacket02 is unaccepted and not runtime-ready.** Its procedural
finish must be baked before any eventual production export.

The bounded vertex study moved upper-garment vertices by at most4.47mm. Saved
source comparison preserves all three LODs' UV/topology/weights/morph deltas,
45 rest bones and13 actions. Protected skin/glove/boot/rubber geometry is unchanged.
The material finish is shared with some trouser/glove detail; it is not described
as a jacket-only material change.

The actual Blender fall audit sampled145 poses per LOD, with minimum floor
clearances2.875/2.986/3.691mm. Those observations do not establish general pose,
skin contact, Unity animation or rendered-player acceptance.

`jacket02-handoff.json` binds41 input/output artifacts, independently rechecked
before publication. Original V7R2, `.blend1`, locked concept and Club hashes match.
Only owned V8 sources, recipes, renders and receipts are published. New tailoring
work uses fresh suffixes; no export, native import, promotion or mask change is
part of this checkpoint.
