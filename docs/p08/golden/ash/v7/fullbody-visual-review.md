# Actual review of the 17 V7 source pose images

All 17 final PNGs in `poses/` were opened and inspected, alongside the final
helmet close views, menu portrait and both R4 hand close views. This records
visual observations rather than another numerical acceptance score.

| Views | Observation |
| --- | --- |
| 01 idle | Coherent body silhouette; jacket, trousers, gloves and boots remain overly smooth, with insufficient folds and worn material detail versus Ash v2. Collar still reads broad. |
| 02–04 menu | The repaired shoulder skin poke is no longer visible. The separate R4 close views show the palm-supported gesture. The isolated figure omits the bike intentionally and is not evidence of floating contact. |
| 05–07 ride/lean | No new gross garment break is apparent in these views. Shoulder/yoke ochre edges remain uneven and overly broad; sleeve/cuff transitions and trouser folds remain weak. These are inherited visual gaps, not accepted finishes. |
| 08–09 attacks | Deformation remains finite/coherent, but gestures are stiff and the hands/cuffs lack the concept's convincing construction. Gameplay actions were preserved, not polished in V7. |
| 10–11 kicks | Leg silhouette stays connected; knee pads and fabric stretch still lack convincing cloth response. The source views omit the bike and opponent. |
| 12 hit | No new visible collar skin poke in this view; the reaction remains a simple inherited pose. |
| 13–15 fall | The inherited fall reads as a rigid body tilt, with weak landing articulation. At the midpoint, a boot visibly enters the fixed review floor; sampled source bounds reach approximately -0.153 m. Actual runtime root/ground handling must be checked before calling this acceptable. The gameplay clip was intentionally not changed under the preservation constraint. |
| 16 run | Coherent sampled pose, but cloth and boot shaping remain below the reference. A single airborne sample does not establish run-cycle quality or foot-contact timing. |
| 17 remount | No obvious new upper-garment rupture; the gesture remains an inherited simplified transition. Runtime alignment to the bike is still separate from the menu-only R4 contact audit. |

The V7 contact fixes and technical exports do not approve these animation,
material or likeness gaps. Final native review and further asset refinement
remain required. The frozen source, FBX and delivery manifest were not changed
by this read-only visual review.
